Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Ry's Tools is a small private set of personal tools (budgeting, seasonal planning and home maintenance tracking) shared with a short list of approved people. This page explains what data it touches and how.

Who Can Use It

Access is limited to Google accounts the site owner has approved. Everyone else who signs in is shown "Access denied" and nothing is loaded for them. Approval is checked by a small server script run by the site owner (see below).

Information We Collect

  • Your Google account email address — used to confirm you're signed in and to check that you're on the approved list. We use Google's userinfo.email scope for this, and nothing else about your Google account.
  • The data you enter — budget figures, seasonal plans, and home maintenance items (titles, costs, dates, notes) and any files you attach to them.
  • Who last saved — for shared Maintenance data, the server records the email of the person who last saved it and when, so simultaneous edits aren't silently overwritten.

How Your Data Is Stored

Maintenance (shared). The data is stored in a file in the site owner's own Google Drive. A Google Apps Script web app, running as the site owner, reads and writes that file. When you sign in, your browser sends Google's sign-in token to that script, which checks it with Google and confirms your email is on the approved list before returning or saving anything. Attachments you add are uploaded through the script into a folder in the owner's Drive that is shared (view only) with the approved people. The script also keeps periodic backup copies of the data file in the owner's Drive. Because it is the owner's Drive and script, the site owner can see everything stored in Maintenance.

Budget and Seasonal (private). These tools store data in your own Google Drive using Google's drive.file scope, the most restrictive Drive scope Google offers. They can only see and edit files they created themselves, and cannot browse any other file in your Drive. They also confirm with the owner's script that your account is approved.

There is no other database. We do not run any servers apart from the Google Apps Script described above, and no copy of your data is kept anywhere else.

What's Stored in Your Browser

  • Your appearance/theme preference (Light, Dark, Elder, Machina).
  • A temporary Google sign-in token, cached only so you don't have to sign in again on every page reload. It expires automatically (about an hour). It is sent only to Google and to the owner's script described above, and it is deleted from your browser if your account isn't approved.

What We Don't Do

Ry's Tools does not use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. Data you enter in Maintenance is visible to the people the owner has approved and to the owner; Budget and Seasonal data stays in your own Drive.

Deleting Your Data

  • For Budget and Seasonal: delete the munny-data.json file from your Google Drive.
  • For Maintenance: ask the site owner (contact below) to remove your entries, or to remove your access.
  • Revoke access for Ry's Tools at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and/or
  • Clear your browser's local storage for this site.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update date above will change. Continued use of Ry's Tools after a change means you accept the updated policy.
